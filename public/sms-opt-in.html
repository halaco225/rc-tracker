Ayvaz RC Tracker — SMS Reminders

Work follow-up reminder texts for Ayvaz Pizza, LLC regional and area coaches.

What you'll receive

  • Reminders about work follow-ups assigned to you (due tomorrow, due today, or overdue)
  • A text when a follow-up is assigned to you
  • Replies when you text the tracker (for example "remind me to call store 39380 Friday" or "1 done")

Sent from (877) 708-9555. Message frequency varies, typically up to a few messages per day. Msg & data rates may apply.

Example:

Ayvaz RC Tracker Your follow-up reminders: 1) Send weekend schedule — due today Reply "done", "Fri", "list", or STOP to opt out

Text message preferences (optional)

Texts are optional. You can use the RC Tracker, and everything in it, without signing up for texts. Only check the box below if you want reminder texts.

Don't want texts? Leave the box unchecked, or just skip this and go to the tracker. You can also sign up later by texting START to (877) 708-9555.

Opting out and help

Text STOP to (877) 708-9555 at any time to stop all messages. Text HELP for help, or email hlacoste@ayvazpizza.com.
